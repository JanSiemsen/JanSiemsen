# Jan Henrik Siemsen

I turn hard geometric and combinatorial problems into exact models and fast code.

I am a computer science master's student at TU Braunschweig, Germany, writing my thesis at Shizuoka University in Hamamatsu, Japan. I graduate in spring 2027.

## What I work on

- **Master's thesis (in progress):** coordinated motion planning for densely packed robots. Robots fill a grid region one per cell and must all reach their targets without collisions, as quickly as possible. I study variants with partially filled regions, unlabeled or colored robots, and regions with holes.
- **[CAGP-Solver](https://github.com/JanSiemsen/CAGP-Solver) (bachelor's thesis):** exact MIP, SAT and CP-SAT solvers for the Chromatic Art Gallery Problem. With SAT, it solves random polygons with 40,000 vertices optimally, 16 times the size of the largest instances tested in prior work.
